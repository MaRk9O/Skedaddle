# Skedaddle

Skedaddle is an outdoor chase game for Android. Host a match, share a six-character code, and use the live map and location pings to find the other players.

**[Download for Android](https://github.com/MaRk9O/Skedaddle/releases/latest)** · [Release notes and older APKs](https://github.com/MaRk9O/Skedaddle/releases)

## Install and update

1. Open the latest release on your Android phone and download **Skedaddle.apk** under **Assets**.
2. Open the downloaded APK and follow Android's prompts. Android may ask you to allow the app you downloaded it with to install APKs.
3. When Skedaddle offers an update, follow the in-app prompts. You can also download the newer APK here and install it over your existing copy.

Skedaddle requires Android 5.0 or newer, location access, and an internet connection for multiplayer. Allow notifications for match alerts and microphone access if you want to use push-to-talk.

## Start a match

One player taps **Host a game** and shares the lobby code. Everyone else taps **Join with a code** and enters it. The host chooses a mode, sets the play area and round length, and starts when at least two players are connected. Tap the mode icon in the lobby for an illustrated guide.

| Mode | Objective |
| --- | --- |
| **Classic** | Hunters use pings to find and tag runners. Runners try to survive until time runs out. |
| **Infection** | Tagged runners become hunters. The hunters try to reach the infection goal before the timer expires. |
| **Teams** | Pick a color, regroup at your team's corner, and be the last team standing. |
| **Elimination** | Every player is on their own. Be the last player standing. |
| **King of the Hill** | Hold the moving hill to score points. Play solo or in teams and finish with the highest score. |

In Teams, Elimination, and King of the Hill, touch an opponent in person, then invite them to a duel. If both accept, play rock, paper, scissors. The runner gets a two-minute head start before the winner can tag them. Then the ten-minute hunt begins. Each pair has its own timer.

Use **Radio** for text and quick phrases. Push-to-talk is available in supported modes when the host enables it. After a match, open **Activity → Match history** to see your results, replay your route, or share a map card.

## Location and data

Skedaddle uses your location to show your position, enforce the play boundary, and share match positions with other players according to the rules of the mode. Location sharing can continue in the background during a match. You can export or restore your settings and match history under **You → Settings**.

The map uses data from [OpenStreetMap contributors](https://www.openstreetmap.org/copyright).
